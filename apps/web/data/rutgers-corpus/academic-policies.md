# Academic policies — student pointers

## Schedule of Classes & registration
- SOC: https://sis.rutgers.edu/soc/
- Course Schedule Planner / WebReg: https://sims.rutgers.edu/webreg/
- Add/drop deadlines are on the academic calendar each term.

## Academic integrity
- Rutgers prohibits submitting work you did not produce for graded assignments.
- Learning Centers and office hours are appropriate support channels.

## Full-time status
- 12+ credits is generally full-time for undergraduates — confirm with RU advising for your school.

## Cross-campus registration
- NB students can have classes on Busch, Livi, C-D, and CA — check meeting campus in SOC before registering.

## Degree requirements
- School-specific (SAS, SOE, RBS). CS majors in SAS use Degree Navigator for the official audit.
