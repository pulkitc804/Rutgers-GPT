## Rutgers New Brunswick Bus Routes

Hand-verified summary of the Rutgers–New Brunswick intercampus bus routes (Passio GO). For live ETAs and the official current schedule, use the Passio GO app or https://rutgers.passiogo.com/ and the official route page https://ipo.rutgers.edu/transportation/buses/nb. The four NB campuses are College Avenue (CA), Busch, Livingston (Livi), and Cook/Douglass (C/D).

Source: https://ipo.rutgers.edu/transportation/buses/nb

## College Avenue to Busch — routes A and H

To get from College Avenue to Busch, take the **A** or the **H** bus — these are the two direct College Avenue ↔ Busch routes.
- **A**: College Avenue Student Center → Hill Center / Science Buildings → Busch Student Center.
- **H**: College Avenue Student Center → Werblin Recreation Center → Busch Student Center → Allison Road Classroom Building.
Both run between the College Avenue Student Center (a common stop) and Busch. Check Passio GO for which is arriving first.

Source: https://ipo.rutgers.edu/transportation/buses/nb

## All NB routes and the campuses they connect

- **A** — College Avenue ↔ Busch (direct).
- **H** — College Avenue ↔ Busch (direct, different path than A).
- **B** — Livingston ↔ Busch (does not serve College Avenue).
- **C** — Busch commuter loop (internal Busch circulation).
- **EE** — College Avenue ↔ Cook/Douglass (via George Street).
- **F** — College Avenue ↔ Cook/Douglass (express via Route 18).
- **LX** — College Avenue ↔ Livingston (express).
- **REXB** — Cook/Douglass ↔ Busch (Route Express Busch).
- **REXL** — Cook/Douglass ↔ Livingston (Route Express Livingston).

Quick reference by trip:
- College Ave ↔ Busch: **A, H**
- College Ave ↔ Livingston: **LX**
- College Ave ↔ Cook/Douglass: **EE, F**
- Livingston ↔ Busch: **B**
- Cook/Douglass ↔ Busch: **REXB**
- Cook/Douglass ↔ Livingston: **REXL**

Routes and stops can change by semester and during breaks/exams — always confirm live times on Passio GO.

Source: https://ipo.rutgers.edu/transportation/buses/nb
