# SAS Computer Science — first-year overview (advisory)

## Typical first Fall (New Brunswick)
- 01:198:111 Introduction to Computer Science (4 cr)
- 01:640:151 Calculus I for Mathematical and Physical Sciences (4 cr) — placement may substitute 152+
- 01:355:101 Expository Writing I (3 cr)
- SAS elective / core (3–4 cr) such as 01:730:101 or another approved course

## Typical first Spring
- 01:198:112 Data Structures (after 111)
- 01:640:152 Calculus II
- 01:355:102 Research in the Disciplines or continuation of writing sequence

## Credit load
- Most first-year CS students target 15–17 credits per semester.
- Dropping below 12 affects full-time status — confirm with advising.

## Always verify
- Degree Navigator (https://dn.rutgers.edu/) is the official degree audit.
- AP credits, honors, and placement tests change this skeleton.
