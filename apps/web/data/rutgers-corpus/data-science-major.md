## Data Science Major (Rutgers–New Brunswick, SAS)

Rutgers–New Brunswick offers a Data Science major through the School of Arts and Sciences (SAS), run jointly by the Department of Statistics, the Department of Computer Science, and partners. It is distinct from the Math major and the Computer Science major. Full, current requirements and the complete course list are on the official Data Science Program site: https://ds.sas.rutgers.edu/. Subject codes: Statistics is 960, Computer Science is 198, Mathematics is 640.

Source: https://ds.sas.rutgers.edu/

## Declaring the Data Science major

As of 2025, you can declare the Data Science major after completing two courses: **Data 101** (Introduction to Data Science) and a **Statistical Inference** requirement. The Statistical Inference requirement can be satisfied by any one of: **01:960:212 Statistics II**, **01:960:384 Intermediate Statistical Analysis**, or **33:136:385 Statistical Methods in Business**. The Data Management course is no longer required to *declare*, but it is still required to *complete* the degree.

Source: https://ds.sas.rutgers.edu/data-science-program/b-a-in-data-science

## B.A. vs B.S. in Data Science

- **B.A. in Data Science** — complete the foundational courses, choose a B.A. degree option/track, and finish the SAS Core Curriculum.
- **B.S. in Data Science** — a more technical track; as of Fall 2024 it includes a **Chemical Data Science** track offered jointly by Computer Science, Statistics, and Chemistry & Chemical Biology.
