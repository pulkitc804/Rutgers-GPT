## How to find your IT support contact

- Search for department and unit IT support contacts by entering the portion of your Rutgers Connect login email address after the @ sign. For example: search for sas.rutgers.edu  if your Rutgers Connect login email address ends in @sas.rutgers.edu.

- You may also search based on the name of your department, unit, or other affiliation.

- Your department/unit IT support can provide you with local assistance to services like Box, Rutgers Connect, and Webex.
