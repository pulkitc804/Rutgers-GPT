# Rutgers–New Brunswick (agent scope)

This product is **New Brunswick only** right now — not Newark or Camden. Within NB there are four campuses students use daily:

## College Avenue (CA)
- Heart of student life; many SAS classes and Expos sections.
- **The Atrium** dining (meal swipe) is inside the **College Avenue Student Center** — official Rutgers Dining places it on the College Avenue campus (replaced Brower Commons while a new dining hall is planned).
- Passio hubs: College Ave Student Center area stops.
- Libraries: Alexander Library.

## Busch
- Engineering, sciences, Busch Dining Hall, Werblin Recreation Center.
- Heavy CS and 640-series math sections often meet here.

## Livingston (Livi)
- Livingston Student Center, Livingston Dining Commons (not the same as The Atrium on College Ave).
- Apartments and recreation; LX buses connect Livi to other campuses.

## Cook/Douglass (C-D)
- Cook Campus: residential, agriculture, environmental science.
- Douglass: Douglass Residential College community.
- Dining halls here are separate from The Atrium (College Ave Student Center).

## Planning tip
- Cross-campus classes are normal; build schedules with travel time between campuses (allow 20–30 min bus buffer).
