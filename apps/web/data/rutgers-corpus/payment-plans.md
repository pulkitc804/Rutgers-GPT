## Rutgers Tuition Payment Plans

- The Rutgers University Tuition Payment Plan (RUTPP) allows you to pay your term bill in monthly payments.

- We have partnered with Nelnet Campus Commerce to allow you to pay your tuition and fees over time in an interest-free plan that automatically deducts the amount from your bank account via e-check payment, credit card, or debit card.

- All card payments incur a 2.45 percent service fee. There is a $55 fee to enroll in RUTPP semester plans, assessed per semester, and a $65 fee to enroll in annual plans. This fee is nonrefundable and due at the time of enrollment.

- To sign up for a payment plan, visit  paymybill.rutgers.edu  and log into your online term bill (OTB). View the step-by-step sign up guide , how-to video , and plan adjustment guide . When enrolling in a tuition payment plan please use a laptop/desktop computer or mobile device with the browser in desktop mode.

- Please contact Nelnet Campus Commerce at 888-470-6014 or view our FAQs below for help with any questions that you may have.

- RBHS students must follow the step-by-step instructional guide to enroll in a payment plan.

**Please Note **

- You will need to log in through the online term bill (OTB) at  paymybill.rutgers.edu  to sign up for a payment plan.

- All down payments are processed immediately.

- Plan payments are automatically processed on the 15th of each month.

- All account balances not paid in full by the due date are the sole responsibility of the student and extend to all costs incurred by the university to collect such debt. This could include collections costs, litigation/attorney's fees, and court costs.

Source: https://finance.rutgers.edu/student-abc/payments/rutgers-tuition-payment-plans

## I am anticipating receiving financial assistance. How much should my plan be?

- Simply deduct the amount of aid or assistance that you expect to receive from the term bill amount and enroll in a plan that accommodates the remaining balance. You can adjust the term bill amount at any time during the plan.

- Please note that you will be responsible for the difference if the aid or assistance is less than anticipated.

Source: https://finance.rutgers.edu/student-abc/payments/rutgers-tuition-payment-plans

## How can I make a payment(s) on a prior term bill?

- Payment plans for prior terms are not available. Please visit the  Delinquent Accounts  page for more information or submit an inquiry to have a representative contact you to make arrangements.

Source: https://finance.rutgers.edu/student-abc/payments/rutgers-tuition-payment-plans

## Can I pay by credit card?

- Yes, however credit card payments incur a 2.45 percent processing fee. This fee is nonrefundable and will appear as a separate transaction on your credit card statement.

Source: https://finance.rutgers.edu/student-abc/payments/rutgers-tuition-payment-plans

- After you have enrolled in RUTPP, please allow three business days for the payment to post to your student account.
