## Why Swim Lessons Are a Lifesaving Skill for Kids in New Jersey

- Summer in New Jersey means beach days, pool time, and family fun, but it also brings an increased risk of water-related incidents. That’s why water safety for kids should be a top priority for families across NJ. One of the most effective ways to keep children safe is through swimming lessons.
