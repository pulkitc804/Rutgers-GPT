# Verified dining locations (New Brunswick)

Primary sources:
- Rutgers Dining **Places to Eat**: https://food.rutgers.edu/places-eat
- Live menus: **FoodPro** at https://menuportal23.dining.rutgers.edu/

## The Atrium
- **Campus:** College Avenue (New Brunswick)
- **Building:** Lower level, College Avenue Student Center (126 College Avenue)
- **Official description:** Rutgers Dining states it is "located inside the College Avenue Student Center" and joins Café West as dining on the College Avenue campus.
- **FoodPro location number:** 13 (menu HTML only — campus label comes from Rutgers Dining pages above, not from FoodPro)
- **Not** on Livingston or Cook/Douglass

## Livingston Dining Commons
- **Campus:** Livingston (New Brunswick)
- **FoodPro location number:** 04
- Separate facility from The Atrium

## How the app uses this
- Preset `atrium` maps to College Avenue + official FoodPro menu URL.
- Preset `livingston-dining` maps to Livingston + its FoodPro URL.
- Menu items are parsed from live HTML; campus assignment uses this verified registry.
